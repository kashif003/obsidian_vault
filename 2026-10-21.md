	 Here is the brief breakdown of the Autoencoder (`Autoencoder`) in `model.py`:

- **Input:** Takes RGB image bands resized to 512x128 pixels (Tensor shape: `(B, 3, 512, 128)`).
    
- **Encoder:** Uses four convolutional and max-pooling blocks to shrink the image into a flattened vector of 8,192 features.
    
- **Latent Space:** A fully connected layer compresses those 8,192 features into a dense **512-dimensional vector**. This specific vector is extracted and sent to the clustering algorithm.
    
- **Decoder:** Expands the 512 dimensions back to 8,192, reshapes it, and uses four transposed convolutional layers to scale it back up to the original image size.
    
- **Output:** A Sigmoid activation function outputs the reconstructed pixel values scaled between 0 and 1.
